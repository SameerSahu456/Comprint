// QUESTIONS WE HEAR FROM EDUCATION & RESEARCH

Asked before. Answered straight.

How do you fit more seats into a fixed grant?
Configuration discipline and commercial options: right-sized specs, rentals where ownership wastes money, and buyback values committed up front. We quote the alternatives side by side.
Can a research group rent GPUs before procurement completes?
Yes. Rented GPU capacity can start the work in days, and convert to purchase later if the project justifies it.
Do you image machines to our lab build?
Yes. Fleets arrive imaged to your standard build with per-department asset registers.
What happens to the old lab when we refresh?
We buy back what has value, redeploy what can serve another campus, and certify-recycle the rest, with documentation for the administration.
