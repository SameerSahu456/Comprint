// QUESTIONS WE HEAR FROM DATA CENTRES & CLOUD

Asked before. Answered straight.

Can you actually get H100-class allocation?
We work NVIDIA and OEM allocation daily and hold what we can secure. Constrained SKUs are a conversation, not a catalogue entry, so bring us the schedule and we will map what is achievable.
Do you supply components at fleet volume?
Yes. Enterprise DRAM, NVMe and drives at volume, priced against a market we track daily and quote with short validity windows to stay honest.
How do fleet-scale refreshes work commercially?
As a programme: staged buyback valuations, redeployment or resale of serviceable nodes, certified disposal for the rest, and credits netted against new supply.
Can delivery match our fill schedule?
Yes. Staggered delivery is planned against your hall-by-hall schedule, with scheduled dispatch from held stock where it exists.
