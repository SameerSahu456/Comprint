// QUESTIONS WE HEAR FROM IT, ITES & GCCS

Asked before. Answered straight.

How fast can you equip a 300-seat ramp?
With stock on hand, imaged fleets begin dispatch in as fast as 48 hours and stagger to your onboarding schedule. Tell us seats, cities and dates and we will commit to a timeline in the quote.
Buy or rent, which is cheaper for us?
It depends on utilisation length. We price both side by side on every quote so the decision is arithmetic, not opinion.
Can you match our global imaging and security build?
Yes. We image to your standard build before dispatch and deliver serialised registers your asset-management system can ingest.
What happens to fleets at the end of a project?
Rented fleets go back to us. Owned fleets can be bought back at assessed values or redeployed to lower-intensity seats.
