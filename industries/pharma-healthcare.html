// QUESTIONS WE HEAR FROM PHARMA & HEALTHCARE

Asked before. Answered straight.

Can you work inside our validation and change-control process?
Yes. Refreshes are planned to your change windows with serial-level documentation, and our engineers work to the runbook your QA team signs off.
How do you handle storage for imaging data growth?
Tiered storage: fast tiers for active studies and imaging, capacity tiers for archives, designed by the practice HPE premiered in 2025. Growth is planned, not panicked.
Can retired devices with patient data be certified destroyed?
Yes. NIST 800-88 sanitisation with per-device certificates and chain-of-custody records, through MPCB and CPCB authorised facilities.
Do you supply for clinical trial sites on short timelines?
Yes. Rented fleets and compute deploy per site and return when the protocol closes, invoiced as opex.
