A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Ask us something else ↗
Can I work with Comprint for just one service?+

Yes. Start with a single purchase, rental, repair or asset recovery requirement. You can connect other lifecycle services when they make sense for your business.

How do I choose between buying, renting and refurbished IT?+

Start with your workload, expected usage period, cash flow and support needs. Our specialists can help compare these options, including a combination of new, rented and refurbished equipment.

Who do I contact if my project spans multiple businesses?+

Start with Comprint. Share the overall project and we’ll help bring together the relevant specialists, so you don’t need to work out the right combination yourself.

Do you support enterprise and multi-location requirements?+

Yes. Share your sites, configurations, rollout schedule and service needs. Delivery, installation and support coverage are scoped for your locations and confirmed in your proposal.

What happens to the technology we are replacing?+

Ecotribe helps assess assets for buyback, reuse and responsible recycling. Data handling, collection, documentation and any recovery value are agreed after reviewing your asset list.

Can resellers and system integrators work with you?+

Yes. Our channel partner programme supports resellers, system integrators and managed service providers. Explore channel partnerships →
