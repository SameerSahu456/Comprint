Buying servers from Comprint

Choosing the right server

Start with the workload. Virtualisation and databases usually suit dual-socket 2U rack servers with plenty of memory. Offices without a server room are often better served by a tower. AI training and inference need multi-GPU platforms planned around power and cooling, while branches and retail sites need compact single-socket or short-depth servers.

New, rental or refurbished

Buy new for the latest platforms and full OEM warranty. Rent when you need capacity for a project, pilot or peak. Choose refurbished previous-generation servers for disaster recovery, test environments and steady workloads at a lower cost.

Configured for your requirement

Every listed price is for the base configuration shown on the product page. Processors, memory, storage, networking, power and support can be changed to suit your workload. Request a quote and we’ll confirm the configuration, availability and delivery timeline.

Frequently asked questions

Yes. Listed prices are for the base configuration shown on each product page. Tell us the processors, memory, storage, networking or support you need and we’ll quote the exact build.

Are prices inclusive of GST?+

Prices are shown excluding GST. GST is added at the applicable rate and shown on your quote and tax invoice.

Do you deliver across India?+

Yes. Delivery charges and timelines depend on the configuration, quantity and location, and are confirmed before you place your order.

Can I rent a server or buy refurbished?+

Many servers are available to rent through Rentr, and refurbished previous-generation servers are available through Refurbr. Choose the option on the product page or ask us to compare.

Do you handle bulk and multi-site orders?+

Yes. Share your quantities, sites and rollout schedule, and we’ll plan configuration, delivery and installation for each location.
