How information is handled when you prepare an enquiry on this website.

Enquiry information

The project brief asks for your name, company, email and project requirements. Phone and location details are optional. Provide only information relevant to the discussion; avoid including passwords, sensitive account details or confidential documents.

Preparing and sending a brief

In this preview, completing the form prepares a brief in your browser. It does not submit an enquiry to a CRM. The email action opens a message for you to review and send using your email application. Downloading a brief saves a text file on your device.

Responding to your enquiry

When you send your enquiry to Comprint, the details you choose to share help the team understand your requirement and connect the relevant specialists. The contact checkbox relates to this enquiry; it is not a newsletter subscription.

Third-party websites

Links to Comprint businesses and other providers take you to their websites. Their forms, accounts and privacy arrangements are managed separately.

Questions about your information

Contact sales@comprint.co.in with questions or requests relating to an enquiry you have shared.
